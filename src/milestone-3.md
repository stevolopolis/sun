# Milestone 3

**Team SUN (Team 7)** · Daeun Park, Abrar Zaki, and Steven Tin Sui Luo

Do similar average temperatures hide different extremes? We compare six weather station pairs using daily observations from 1973–2024. This comparison starts with daily distributions and seasonal cycles, then asks how often extremes occur, how long they last, and how they change over time.

[Project introduction](./milestone-3/intro) · [Data & Methods](./milestone-3/data-methods)

## Domain questions

1. [Temperature Distributions](./milestone-3/q1) compares the spread and median of daily highs and lows.
2. [Seasonal Patterns](./milestone-3/q2) compares monthly temperature cycles for stations with similar long-term means.
3. [Extreme Frequency](./milestone-3/q3) asks how often the stations have very hot days or freezing nights.
4. [Extreme Duration](./milestone-3/q4) asks how long temperature extremes last at each station.
5. [Extreme Trends](./milestone-3/q5) asks how extremes and differences between station averages have changed over time.
6. [Seasonal Transitions](./milestone-3/q6) asks how stations differ in their transitions between seasons.

Q1–Q4 have interactive D3 charts, source code, and explanations. Q5–Q6 currently contain the research questions and section headings.
