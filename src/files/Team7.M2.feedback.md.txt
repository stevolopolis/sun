## Points to think through before Milestone 3

### Data handling & understanding

1. **Turn the descriptions into processed tables.** M2 asks for transformed results for every question. A merged station-day file can support several questions, but it does not demonstrate quartiles, monthly means, valid-day rates, spell lengths, or transition dates. Show a few real rows of each derived output, or a shared table whose fields clearly support them.
2. **Make missingness part of the calculations.** Keeping original NOAA sentinels in raw files is fine; convert them to missing before deriving statistics. Document field-specific markers and valid-day denominators. A missing calendar day must not silently join two extreme spells.
3. **Define thresholds and incomplete years.** Q3 uses roughly 90°F/32°F while Q4 suggests 90°F/−10°F, and it is unclear whether MAX, MIN, or TEMP determines a spell. Choose exact inequalities and explain differing definitions. Do not compare partial 2025 annual counts with full earlier years without a matched window or valid-day rate.
