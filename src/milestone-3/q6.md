### What the visualization shows

**Some pairs exhibit significant differences in daily temperature fluctuations**
Pairs such as Arcata/Des Moines, San Francisco/St. Louis, Addis Ababa/Atlanta, and Reykjavik/Caribou exhibit large differences between their largest absolute deviation of daily temperature from their 15-day average temperature, despite sharing the same average yearly average temperature. This is reflected by the large gap between some of the red/blue line pairs in the figures. This shows that some locations in these pairs exhibit much larger and sharper temperature variations on a daily basis where one day may be significantly colder or significantly warmer than the general trend of temperatures. 

**Daily temperature fluctuations are generally consistent across the years for all locations**
There is no significant global trends observable for each of the red nor blue lines, indicating that the largest absolute deviation in daily temperature from the 15-day average has not increased nor decreased generally across the years for all locations. 

**Christchurch's abnormal years: 1993-1994**
Christchurch is the only location that exhibited extremely abnormal patterns between the years 1993 and 1994, where its largest absolute deviation went from its general average of around 15F to suddenly almost 35F. This is reflected by the sharp spike for the red line in the Seattle/Christchurch figure.
