### What the visualization shows

Comparing the dotted and solid green lines across all the figures, we can see that the average temperatures across all pairs have remained similar across the years (1973-2024). Only slight deviations can be observed at a small set of years, but there is no global trend of divergence.

To study the trend of extreme temperatures, we plot the yearly maximum and yearly minimum temperatures against the year. Generally speaking, the trends of both of these metrics across the pairs are quite flat, indicating that the yearly maximum and minimum temperatures have not shown significant increases or decreases within the period 1973-2024. However, we can observe slight global increases in the yearly maximum temperatures from Tucson/New Orleans, and slight global increases in the yearly minimum temperatures from Reykjavik/Caribou and San Francisco/St. Louis.

Lastly, the gap between the pairs' yearly maximum and yearly minimum temperature are also held generally constant across the years. The only exception is Reykjavik/Caribou which has a slightly converging yearly minimum temperature as shown by their converging dotted and solid blue lines.
