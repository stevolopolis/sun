# Intro

**Team SUN (Team 7)** · Daeun Park, Abrar Zaki, and Steven Tin Sui Luo

Do similar average temperatures hide different extremes? Compare six pairs of weather stations using daily observations from 1973–2024.

Milestone 1 proposed comparing temperature, precipitation, wind, and other weather variables across regions. Milestone 2 narrowed the project to six pairs of stations with similar long-term mean temperatures so we could compare their daily distributions, seasonal patterns, and extremes directly.
