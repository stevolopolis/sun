### Visualization

### What the visualization shows

Arcata and Des Moines differ by only **0.47 °F** in long-term mean temperature. The difference between the warmest and coldest monthly means is **10.07 °F** in Arcata and **54.55 °F** in Des Moines. The calendar alignment also shows opposite peak timing for Seattle and Christchurch, whose warmest months are **August** and **January**, respectively.
